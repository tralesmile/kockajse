# Lessons

Evidence-based lessons from the review task. Apply them every day.

- Bookmaker prices are very accurate. In 42,000 European league matches (2019-2026,
  `data/backtest/`), the most likely ticket chosen from the market's chances alone won 37.2%
  of the time against 36.3% predicted. Only change a market chance when the research finds
  concrete, recent, decisive news (a confirmed absence of a key player, a rested lineup,
  nothing at stake), never because of general form or reputation.
- Strong favourites win slightly more often than their price says (favourite-longshot bias):
  teams the de-margined price gives 70-80% won about 3 points more often than predicted.
  Do not mark a clear favourite RISKY only because its price is short; do treat long
  prices with extra caution.
